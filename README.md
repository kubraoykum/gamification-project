# gamification-project
🏰 The Architects of Avalon — Data Modeling Quest (COM4061)
Developed within the scope of Istanbul Kültür University, The Architects of Avalon is a web-based educational gamification project. Set in a fantasy atmosphere, it teaches database analysis, entity discovery, cardinality, and normalization concepts (1NF, 2NF, 3NF).

🕹️ How to Play
Entry & Class Selection: Click the play link above to start the adventure. Enter a pseudonymous Player Code, choose your difficulty level, and select a Guardian Class (Scout, Builder, Time-Keeper, Data Sage) that suits your modeling style.

Chapter 1 (Library of Entities): Discover the independent Entities of the scenario, assign a unique Primary Key to each, and drag-and-drop the Attributes into their correct tables.

Chapter 2 (Bridge Building Quest): Determine the numerical nature of the relationships between entities (1:1, 1:N, M:N). Use the Junction Stone to create associative bridge tables for many-to-many (M:N) relationships.

Chapter 3 (Purification Combat): Battle the Redundancy Monster, which feeds on duplicate and inconsistent data! Defeat the boss by correctly identifying 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF dependency violations to restore the Avalon archives.

No installation or download required; you can play directly on any modern browser using your mouse or via touch controls on mobile devices.

💎 Core Gamification & Pedagogical Features
GPAF & Log Integration: Features action-based anonymous .jsonl game logs fully compatible with both team and instructor evaluation frameworks.

Interactive ERD Canvas: An SVG-based, dynamic drag-and-drop interface that allows players to design and link their data models visually.

Kirkpatrick Evaluation Model: Measures your progress throughout the session using Pre-Test and Post-Test (L2) metrics, coupled with a satisfaction survey (L1) at the end.

Mastery Radar Chart: A personalized post-session diagnostic report analyzing your data modeling competencies (Varlık, PK, Cardinality, 2NF, 3NF) across a 5-dimension chart.

Grandmaster's Vault: An in-game reward shop where you can spend your earned Logic Runes on integrity potions and custom cosmetic upgrades.
